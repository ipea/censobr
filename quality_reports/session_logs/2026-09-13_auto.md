2. `:209` `expect_true(is(df_y, "ArrowObject"))` — never passed; introduced in `03e77fe`.
   `dplyr::select()` on an arrow `Dataset` returns an `arrow_dplyr_query` (S3), not an R6
   `ArrowObject`. True for **every** year with `columns=`, not just 1980/1991. Every other
   `ArrowObject` assertion in the suite sits on a `columns = NULL` call.

**Decision:** fix the tests, not the code. Returning a lazy query after `select()` is the
larger-than-memory contract; forcing a real `Dataset` would materialise the projection.

moved to genuinely unlabelled years (1991, 1970), matching on 'only available'.
